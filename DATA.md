# Filming guide

## What makes a clip analyzable
- **Player fills at least ~⅓–½ of the picture height.** At 120–170 px tall (far away, or a compressed 540×960 file) most arm checks can't be measured.
- **Original file:** AirDropped or picked straight from the camera roll. Texting a video shrinks it to 540×960.
- **60 fps:** at 30 fps the racket arm blurs at contact, and pronation can't be measured at all.
- **Phone fixed:** handheld, panning or SwingVision-style auto-zoom turns off the foot-fault and court checks.
- **Any view works for the motion checks:** behind, behind-diagonal, side, front, front-diagonal. Foot faults need the baseline corners visible and a still phone.

## Recommended setup
| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Camera position | **Rear-oblique:** ~4 m behind the baseline, ~2 m to the side of the server (away from the hitting arm), pointing toward the net |
| Height | ~1.2 m (fence, bag, or tripod), phone steady, not handheld |
| Orientation | Landscape |
| Frame rate | **60 fps** (iPhone: Settings → Camera → Record Video → 1080p at 60 fps). Not slo-mo. |
| Framing | Whole body, both baseline corners and both service-line corners of the server's half in the shot |
| Clip | From ~1 s before the toss to ~1 s after landing |
| Lighting | Daylight, not filming into the sun |

Straight-behind works for phases and most motion checks, but foot faults more often come back "Can't tell" because the back leg can hide the front foot.
